# Betrayed Welcome Messages

These templates use placeholders that Zors Bot will replace with Discord mentions and member information.

## Public welcome channel

Zors Bot shuffles 20 one-sentence greetings and uses every greeting once before
reshuffling. Every greeting mentions the new member and welcomes them to
**Betrayed**.

## Direct message from Zors Bot

Welcome to **Betrayed**, {{displayName}}!

We're glad you're here.

If you have any questions regarding Betrayed as a whole, please message **@Zors** or any of the **@Officers** online in Discord and one will happily assist you.

Interested in raiding on one of Betrayed's raid teams? Use **Apply to the Raid Teams** below to open the application page.

Joining us for a PUG? Join any Betrayed voice channel first, then use **Move Me to PUG Voice** below.

Here to look around and meet the community? Use **Explore Forever Discussion** below to jump into the conversation.

We're happy to have you here. Enjoy the server!

— **Zors Bot**

The three action buttons appear beneath this message. Zors Bot then sends a
second DM containing the clickable Zors profile link and profile preview so the
action buttons remain above the profile card.
